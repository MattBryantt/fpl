Fixtures & team strength

Every match in the horizon, with the expected goals each side is scored against and the clean sheet chance that implies. Priced fixtures come from a bookmaker's odds; modelled ones are estimated from attack/defence ratings because no market was found for that match. Type over either xG figure to try your own view — new signing, a manager sacked, a keeper injured — and every player in that match rescores immediately, everywhere on the board. Calibrate to odds nudges a modelled fixture's own club toward what the books said about that same club's priced fixtures elsewhere in the horizon -- a transfer or a managerial change the ratings cannot see yet. It never touches a fixture that is itself priced.

Nearly in

Who the optimal squad leaves out by the smallest margin. Each candidate is forced in and the other fourteen are rebuilt around him, so the gap already charges him for the money he ties up — a gap of zero means a squad built around him scores exactly as well and the solver chose between them on a tie. This is the question the ranking cannot answer: a player can be fourth in his position on points and nowhere near the squad because everyone above him is cheaper, and another can be twentieth and one swap away.

Team lineups

Who the model expects to start, and for how long. Minutes are a fixed pool — eleven players start — so overriding one man's start probability takes the minutes off his team-mates rather than inventing a twelfth starter. Each row is ordered left-to-right by tagged position where one is set; use ‹ › on a shirt to correct it — the shape is remembered per club.

Transfers & chips

What each chip is worth to a squad built for it. Every figure here comes from a fifteen bought from scratch over the whole horizon, on your budget — which is also the wildcard draft: after Sync the budget is what selling your fifteen raises plus the bank, and your banked free transfers carry over the rebuild — knowing which chip is coming and when — because that is the decision: a bench boost is only worth playing if you spend on a bench worth fielding, and pricing it against the cheap bench you happen to own answers a different question. Each chip is scored against the same money spent with no chip at all. This tab does not read the squad you own, so it will not tell you how to get there from where you are — that is the Squad tab's job.
These are the hand-set constants the plan reserves a chip or a banked transfer against — see the note under the table below for what they mean. Dragging one re-prices every chip; press Re-plan to see the new answer.
